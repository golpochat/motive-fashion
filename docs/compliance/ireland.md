# Ireland / EU compliance checklist

Not legal advice. Confirm with an Irish solicitor and accountant before go-live.

- [ ] CRO company (Ltd) and registered office
- [ ] VAT registration (standard clothing rate 23% unless a relief applies)
- [ ] Prices shown VAT-inclusive
- [ ] Distance selling: 14-day withdrawal, returns policy at `/legal/returns`
- [ ] Terms, privacy, cookies pages live
- [ ] GDPR: lawful basis, DSR export/delete (`/account/gdpr-export`)
- [ ] WhatsApp and email marketing: opt-in only
- [ ] Cookie banner for non-essential cookies
- [ ] EAA accessibility for eCommerce (keyboard, contrast, alt text)
- [ ] Stripe Ireland entity / EUR settlement
- [ ] Consumer Rights Act information duties at checkout

Supplier seed contacts are **examples**. Do not outreach until verified; treat personal numbers as personal data.
